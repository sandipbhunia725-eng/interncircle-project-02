I created Project 02 – Number Guessing Game & Word Counter using Python.

The guessing game selects a random number from 1 to 100, gives the player seven valid attempts, provides higher/lower hints, and tracks a score and best score.

Scoring rule: The player starts with 100 points and loses 10 points for each incorrect valid guess. The score cannot go below zero. Invalid inputs and out-of-range guesses do not use an attempt.

The word counter reads a UTF-8 text file, ignores case and punctuation, and uses collections.Counter to display total words, unique words, and the ten most common words.
